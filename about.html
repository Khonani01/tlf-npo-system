About Tshwane Leadership Foundation

The Tshwane Leadership Foundation is a non-profit organisation dedicated to empowering communities, developing leadership skills, and supporting social development initiatives across Tshwane.

Through partnerships, community programs, and educational initiatives, the foundation works to uplift individuals and create sustainable positive change in society.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities through leadership development, education, and social programs that create long-term positive impact in Tshwane.

Our Vision

To build strong and self-sustaining communities where people are equipped with leadership skills, opportunities, and resources to thrive.
