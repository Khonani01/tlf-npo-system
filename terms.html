Terms and Conditions

Please read these terms before applying to volunteer with the Tshwane Leadership Foundation (TLF).

Last updated: October 2026

1. About these terms

These terms apply when you submit a volunteer application on this website. By ticking "I agree to the terms and conditions" and submitting the form, you confirm that you have read and accepted them.

2. Who can apply

  • You must be 18 years or older. If you are younger, a parent or guardian must contact TLF before you apply.
  • The information you give us must be true and up to date.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee a volunteer placement. TLF reviews each application and may approve or decline it.

3. What we ask of volunteers

  • Treat community members, staff and other volunteers with respect and dignity.
  • Attend the events and programmes you sign up for, or tell TLF as early as possible if you cannot.
  • Follow TLF's safety instructions and the rules of each event or venue.
  • Keep personal information you learn about community members confidential.
  • Represent TLF honestly and do not speak on its behalf unless you are authorised to.

4. Volunteering is unpaid

Volunteer work is given freely and is unpaid. Volunteering does not make you an employee of TLF. Any costs TLF agrees to cover, such as transport or meals, will be confirmed with you beforehand.

5. Your personal information

TLF respects your privacy and handles your information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

  • What we collect: your full name, email address, phone number, your skill area, the event or programme you choose, and the optional message you write.
  • Why we collect it: only to review your application, contact you about volunteering, and organise TLF events and programmes.
  • Who sees it: authorised TLF administrators. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.
  • How we protect it: your application is stored in a secured database and only accessible to administrators who log in.
  • How long we keep it: only for as long as it is needed for volunteering, after which it is deleted.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

6. Ending your involvement

You may stop volunteering at any time. TLF may end a volunteer's involvement if these terms or its safety rules are not followed.

7. Changes to these terms

TLF may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when they were last changed.

8. Contact us

Questions about these terms or your information? Email info@tlfoundation.org or phone +27 12 345 6789.
